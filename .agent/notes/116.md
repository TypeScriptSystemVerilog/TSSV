## The contract, as the human stated it

Their words: "the request is always a single cycle pulse that gets captured into the down stream
slave on the next clk edge. The interface is not pipelined and there is only one outstanding
transaction at a time. Ready should be high only during all cycles where the bus is holding the
last settled access. So it seems the ready as drawn falls a cycle early." They confirmed:

- `ADDR` is a byte address.
- A zero-wait slave can keep `READY` high through an access. `READY` drops only for a slave that
  inserts wait states, starting in the cycle after the request.
- `DATA_RD` is valid while `READY` is high after a read, and holds until the next request.

They asked whether the diagrams need annotations for cause and effect and for slave-side wait
injection. The proposal is WaveDrom `node`/`edge` arrows from the request to its capture edge,
and a marked wait-state span. Show the diagrams to the human before finalizing them.
