## Dead ends
- Moving a constant that two scopes share out to the scope that encloses both: for two sibling
  views that scope is the top level, where the constant is unused in a build without either
  view, and `-Wall` fails (`UNUSEDSIGNAL`). Replaced by: move out only when the new use's scope
  encloses the old one; otherwise an auto-named constant gets a copy, `<name>_v2`.
- SV-2023 `` `ifdef (A || B) ``: Verilator 5.052 accepts it, but `verible-verilog-syntax`
  rejects it and the formatter fails with `--failsafe_success=false`. So a list of views
  repeats the branch under `` `ifdef `` / `` `elsif ``.
