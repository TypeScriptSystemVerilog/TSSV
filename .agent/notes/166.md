## Assumptions

- The check errs towards accepting where the tree or the signal table doesn't say enough:
  - A reset branch under `case`, or under a condition other than a plain test of the reset,
    isn't found (SEQ-6, and "uses a reset" for RST-2 and SEQ-5).
  - A reset not marked `isReset` isn't a reset. As a sync reset, it's invisible. In an event
    control, SEQ-4 counts it as a second clock, which is the intended error.
  - RST-4 can't see resets generated in another module, in imported SV, or carried in an
    interface.
  - COMB-3 treats `always @(a or b)` as combinational, so a latch written that way with `<=`
    would fail. BUILD-4 already forbids `inputs` lists.
- `addSubmodule` only binds signal names; a bigint becomes a constant signal. So RST-4's
  "bound to an expression" branch is defensive.
- The tree tags (`kEventExpression`, `kContinuousAssignmentStatement`, `kNetDeclarationAssignment`,
  `kGateInstance`, ...) were checked on the pinned Verible `v0.0-4296-g0f262651`. A new pin may
  rename them; `node out/test/test_moduleBody.js` would show it.
