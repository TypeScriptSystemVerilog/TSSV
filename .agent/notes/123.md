Three rules were changed from the issue as first filed, each by the human in this session and
written into the issue body:

- **Hash:** 41-bit SHA-256 in 8 base-36 characters, not 40-bit base32.
- **Strings:** any 1 to 16 characters from `[A-Za-z0-9_$]`, so `'1rw'` is appended, and
  `{ w: 8 }` / `{ w: '8' }` both giving `X_8` is accepted.
- **Bigints:** written as integers in canonical JSON.
