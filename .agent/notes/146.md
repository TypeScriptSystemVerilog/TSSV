## Dead ends

- Operand casts to the result's width, `W'(a) op W'(b)`: the reviewer rejected them (comment
  6175451752). Don't go back to them.
- A literal at the width the operation computes at (`a + 9'd5` for an 8-bit `a`): replaced by
  literals at their own width, at Tom Richardson's request.
- Moving a negative literal's sign into the operation (`a + -8` written as `a - 5'sd8`, `a * -8`
  as `-(a * 5'sd8)`): Tom Richardson rejected it. Keep the sign as written.
- `-4'sd8` for -8: `4'sd8` is already -8, so the negation gives +8. Size the magnitude with a
  sign bit: `-5'sd8`.
- `sa < 1'sd0`: raises `Operator LTS` `WIDTHEXPAND`. Write `sa < W'(1'sd0)`.
- A `*` in a rule's bold title in `sv-coding-style.md`: the heading regex in
  `doc/rtl-style/sv-style/tools/check-rules.mjs` is `[^*]*`, so the rule isn't found. That's
  why WIDTH-3's title says "add, subtract and multiply".
- A testbench that ties an asynchronous `rst_n` low from time zero never resets: there is no
  negedge. Start `rst_n` at 1 and drop it, as `check-builder-widths.mjs` does for clocked cases.
- Counting `FAIL:` lines from `check-builder-widths.mjs` over-counts failing cases. One case can
  fail both lint and values. Count distinct case names.
- A test can't read `Module.IOs` or `Module.signals`: both are protected. That's why
  `test_builder_widths.ts` has its `WidthCase` subclass.
- In the test, the `Port` type's `signed` field isn't `Module`'s `isSigned`. Map it when building
  a leaf module.
